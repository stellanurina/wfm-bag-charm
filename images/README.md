# Images Needed

Place the following images here before deploying:

## Required

- [ ] `wfm-logo.png` — WFM logo with white/pink/teal flower mark and text. PNG with transparent background. Min height: 40px.
- [ ] `charm-product.jpg` — Product photo of the paracord bag charm. Landscape orientation works best for the hero. High resolution. The NADYA charm photo from your reference images is a great choice.
- [ ] `favicon.ico` — Small WFM logo favicon, 16x16 or 32x32.

## Notes for Designer

- `wfm-logo.png` should work on both light and dark backgrounds (white or transparent BG preferred).
- `charm-product.jpg` should show the charm clearly — the colorful paracord and letter beads are the hero.
- Hero image is optional; if omitted, the hero section will display without a photo and show the gradient background only.
